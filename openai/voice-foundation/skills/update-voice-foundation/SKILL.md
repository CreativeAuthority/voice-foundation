name: update-voice-foundation
description: "Update my saved Voice Foundation when I ask to change enduring business values, decision rules, or voice patterns; regenerate my private skill and PDF."
---

Use for an explicit durable update, not every edit to a draft.

Locate the foundation for the current agent/business and its source JSON/checkpoint. Read the existing content first; do not replace another business or an unrelated skill with the same generic name. If the source is unavailable, recover from the user's exported foundation and verify affected evidence; do not reconstruct missing quotes as if verbatim.

Ask one targeted question if the requested permanent change is ambiguous. Distinguish a new lasting preference from an exception for this piece. Preserve unrelated mission, decision rules, and the separate written/spoken systems. New anchors must come from the agent; never use your own draft as evidence of their natural voice.

Create a new revision with the same agent_id and incremented revision. Record the explicit correction as new evidence with a supersedes pointer to the prior evidence, retaining both, update affected rules/patterns, and invalidate their old review status until the agent has seen and confirmed the changed material. Show a concise before/after for their review, not the whole interview again.

Use [delivery](references/delivery.md) to regenerate the unified foundation, PDF, portable instructions, and personalized skill from the same revised source. Preserve the older deliverables as a previous revision. Update the existing personalized skill for this agent instead of installing a second competing skill; read back its reference and verify the installed revision. If the host requires reinstallation or a new conversation to pick up changes, say so clearly. Do not claim success from writing a file outside a discoverable skill location.
