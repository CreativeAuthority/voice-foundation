# Session 2: written and spoken voice

Use only after the mission review. Keep the two channels separate in every part. Ask for existing emails before analyzing written patterns; ask for an existing transcript or dictated example before analyzing spoken patterns. Remind the agent they may remove client identifiers. Redactions stay redactions in the anchors.

## Audience and intent
For each channel establish a specific audience and context, the feeling to leave, and the action or emotional outcome. Ask these across turns, not bundled. Narrow “buyers and sellers” to a recent recipient or situation.

## Trust and authority
Written: what the sample leads with, what it refuses to claim, and how it earns credibility. Spoken: actual opening, handling skepticism, and moves the agent would never use. Get the real line, not a sales-coach ideal. Read inferred patterns back for confirmation.

## Explaining trade-offs
Written: paste an actual prior explanation. Spoken: ask them to dictate how they handled a difficult trade-off or provide the real transcript. Collect two or three objections with their responses, one at a time. Record whether a response is an actual recollection or a proposed future response. Do not polish dialogue.

## Language and rhythm
For each channel extract phrases used, words/constructions avoided, sequence, rhythm, openings and closings. Confirm observations. Ask for concrete examples of AI drift: a word, formula, sentence shape, or rhetorical move that signals “not me.” A written failure signature and a spoken failure signature are both required. Different registers can coexist when their usage is labeled.

## Permissions
What may AI help do with writing? What must it never change? Ask the equivalent for scripts on a separate turn. Establish a concrete uncertainty behavior. Route decisions about what the agent will do to the mission/rules section with their review; voice permissions focus on expression.

## Anchors
At least one existing written sample and one real spoken sample, each with a source label and the agent's confirmation. An existing 6–12 sentence written excerpt is a useful target, not a mechanical cap. Preserve exact text. Typed recollections can inform questioning but cannot silently satisfy the spoken-transcript requirement. Do not generate an “authentic” anchor on their behalf. An optional failed example can sharpen the failure signature.

## Read-back and finish
Show written rules, patterns, exact anchor, and failure signature; show spoken rules, patterns, objections, exact anchor, and failure signature separately. Incorporate corrections. Then show the combined foundation and obtain one final accuracy confirmation. Refer to [delivery](delivery.md) to generate all outputs and register the private writing skill. Do not stop with the profile text alone.
